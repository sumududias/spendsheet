SpendSheet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

SpendSheet ("the app") is an offline expense tracker published by Sumudu Dias. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information you enter

Income and expense records, categories, your name, organisation name and report logo are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not operate any server that receives this information, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

The app never asks for bank account, credit card or debit card numbers.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or financial information. The app has no user accounts, analytics or tracking.

3. Backups and exported files

When you create a backup, PDF report or CSV file, it is saved or shared only to the place you choose (for example your phone's storage, Google Drive or email). Those services handle the file under their own privacy policies.

If Android's built-in device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

4. App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored on the device only as a salted one-way hash. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only to open links (such as this policy) when you tap them.
  • Biometric: used only if you enable fingerprint or face unlock.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos or files. Files are chosen through Android's system file picker, which only gives the app the file you select.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Deleting your data

Use Settings > Clear All Data in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Changes

If this policy changes (for example if advertising is added to a free version), the updated policy will be published at this address with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
